# Cancellation and Returns

<p class="lp-legal-note">This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If there is any difference between the two, the Portuguese version prevails.</p>

**1.** The cancellation or return process is handled case by case by Liliana Pona Jewellery. The request must be made in writing to the contact email [liliana.pona@lilianaponajewellery.pt](mailto:liliana.pona@lilianaponajewellery.pt) or [vitor.sarmento@lilianaponajewellery.pt](mailto:vitor.sarmento@lilianaponajewellery.pt), up to 30 days after the order, and the instructions for cancellation or refund of the amount paid and/or return of the goods will be answered and defined by Liliana Pona Jewellery through the same means of communication.

**2.** Whenever possible the refund should be made through the same payment method; if that is not possible, the user must present proof of payment and of ownership of the account or card, so that the refund can be made by Bank Transfer.

**3. Liliana Pona Jewellery** undertakes to refund the user after receiving the returned order.

**4.** Returned products must be in saleable condition, that is, in the same condition in which they reached the User, without any anomaly or damage.

This right of return applies only to products in good condition and complete (packaging, accessories, manual, etc.) (cf. Article 6 of Decree-Law no. 82/2008 of 20 May).

**5.** If the value resulting from the exchange of products in an order is lower or higher than its initial value, the settlement conditions will be indicated by Liliana Pona Jewellery.

**6. The buyer bears the cost of shipping the returned goods.**

## Article 7 – Privacy Policy

**1.** Your data is processed in compliance with the legislation on the protection of personal data. It is subject to computer processing and encryption, will be held in the database(s) of Liliana Pona Jewellery, and is intended for registration and for the presentation of other products and services, as well as institutional information, to be made available by the same.

**2.** Providing it is optional, and the right of access, rectification and cancellation of any data that directly concerns you is guaranteed, under the terms of the law, in person or in writing, directly to the address shown on the home page of this website.
