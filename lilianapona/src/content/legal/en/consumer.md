# Consumer information

## Assay office and hallmarking

A hallmark is a State guarantee of the amount of precious metal in the alloy. Below is the information the law requires us to publish.

<p class="lp-legal-note">This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If there is any difference between the two, the Portuguese version prevails.</p>

### Products

All products created, produced and sold by LILIANA PONA JEWELLERY are genuine and carry the brand’s Guarantee of Authenticity.

All products created, produced and sold by LILIANA PONA JEWELLERY comply with Portuguese law.

Articles containing precious metal are legally hallmarked under Law no. 98/2015 whenever the law requires it: the assay office mark and the responsibility (maker’s) mark.

### Finenesses we sell

The law measures fineness in parts per thousand: the parts of precious metal in every thousand parts of the alloy, by weight. Carats, the measure most often used for gold, are shown alongside.

- **Gold 800‰** – 19.2 ct. The fineness of Portuguese gold.
- **Gold 750‰** – 18 ct.
- **Gold 375‰** – 9 ct.
- **Silver 925‰** – sterling silver, also in our gilded and oxidised silver pieces.

The product page of each piece states its metal and fineness.

### Checking a piece

If you have any doubt about the authenticity of a hallmark, you can ask the Assay Offices (Contrastarias) of the Portuguese Mint and Official Printing Office (INCM) to check it. All the information is at [contrastaria.pt](https://contrastaria.pt/en/consumer-information/).

### Metal prices

Under articles 63 and 64 of Law no. 98/2015 of 18 August (RJOC), the daily prices of gold, silver, platinum and palladium can be consulted on the [Banco de Portugal](https://www.bportugal.pt/) website and at [bullion-rates.com](https://www.bullion-rates.com/).
